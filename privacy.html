Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Watch List is a free, ad-free app made by an independent developer (Sylvain G.). This page explains which data the app uses, why, and how to delete it. Questions: watchlist.query@gmail.com.

In short

  • Without an account, your lists stay on your phone only.
  • With an account (optional), they are backed up in the cloud so you find them on your devices.
  • Usage statistics are off by default and only sent with your consent.
  • No data is sold, and the app shows no ads.
  • You can delete your account and backups at any time from the app.

Your lists

The movies and shows you add, your ratings, viewing dates, personal notes, custom lists, Top 5, avatar, profile name and settings are stored on your device. Without an account, they don't leave your phone, unless you choose to export or share them (a file or QR code made on the device).

Account and cloud backup

Creating an account is optional. It backs up your lists and syncs them between your devices.

  • Sign-in: with an email and password, or with Google or Apple, through Firebase Authentication (Google). Firebase receives your email address and an account identifier; with Google or Apple, also the associated name. Your password is handled by Firebase; the app doesn't store it.
  • Backup: a copy of your lists (the content described above) is saved in Firebase Cloud Storage (Google), in a space only your account can access.
  • Retention: the backup is kept as long as your account exists, and deleted with it.

Usage statistics

Only with your consent, the app sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: screens viewed, features used, technical device information and a pseudonymous identifier. They are only used to improve the app. They are off by default; you can change your mind at any time in Settings > Privacy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a technical report: device model, system and app version, and where in the code it happened. It doesn't contain your lists. These reports are only used to fix bugs.

Movies and shows (TMDB)

Movie and show information comes from The Movie Database (TMDB). When you search for a title or open one, the app queries TMDB: the request contains your search or the title's identifier, and TMDB sees your IP address, as any website does. No TMDB account is used, and your lists aren't sent to TMDB. See TMDB's privacy policy.

Import from another app

The export file you import (TV Time, Letterboxd, IMDb) is read on your device and sent nowhere. Only the titles it contains are searched on TMDB to find them.

Notifications

Reminders (a movie's release, your watching streak…) are scheduled on your device. No remote notification service is used.

Sharing and transfers

Data is only shared with the providers above (Google for Firebase, TMDB), to make the app work. Google may process it outside the European Union, with the safeguards of its terms (standard contractual clauses). No data is sold or used for advertising.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data:

  • Export: Settings > My data > Export my Watch List.
  • Delete your account and backups: Profile > My account > Delete my account. Deletion is permanent.
  • Withdraw consent to statistics: Settings > Privacy.
  • Delete the data on the device: uninstall the app.

For any other request, write to watchlist.query@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child sent us data, write to us to have it deleted.

Changes

This policy may change with the app. The date at the top of the page shows its last update.
